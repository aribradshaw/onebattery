# OneBattery privacy policy

**App:** OneBattery by Flygon LC (`com.flygonlc.onebattery`)
**Developer:** Flygon LC
**Contact:** info@flygonlc.com
**Effective date:** 11 November 2026

## The short version

OneBattery does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. Everything it records stays on your device. The app does not have Android's internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone.

## What the app reads, and where it stays

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Battery level, charging state, current, voltage, temperature, health, cycle count | To show battery status and history, and to measure your battery's real capacity | Stored in the app's private database on your device |
| Which app is in the foreground, and Android's per-app records of foreground-service time and background data usage. Only if you grant **Usage access** | To estimate which apps drain your battery | Turned into per-app battery totals and stored in the app's private database on your device. No browsing history, content, messages or network destinations are read. |
| Names of installed apps | To label the per-app drain list | Shown on screen only, not stored beyond the package names in the drain totals |
| Your settings (theme, notification options, design capacity) | To remember your choices | The app's private storage on your device |

Raw readings and per-app totals are deleted automatically after one year. Capacity measurements are kept so the long-term health trend stays available. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes everything.

## What the app does not do

- No accounts, sign-in, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or third-party SDKs that send data.
- No location, contacts, camera, microphone, files, photos or phone identifiers.
- No data is sold or shared with anyone, including Flygon LC. We never receive it.

## Device backup

OneBattery's history database (battery readings, capacity measurements and, if you allowed Usage access, per-app usage and drain history) is **excluded** from Android backup and from device-to-device transfer, so it never leaves your phone. It describes this phone's battery, so it would be wrong on another phone anyway.

Only the app's settings (theme, design capacity, notification options) may be included in **your own** device backup to your Google account, if you have backups turned on. That backup is controlled by Google and by you, is end-to-end encrypted on supported devices, and is not accessible to Flygon LC. You can turn it off in Android Settings.

## Permissions

The app's permissions are explained in plain language in the [README](README.md#permissions-and-why-each-is-needed). Usage access is optional and can be revoked at any time in **Settings → Apps → Special app access → Usage access**.

## Children

OneBattery is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Because the app collects no data, a change could only be needed if a future version adds a feature that does. Any such feature would be disclosed before release.
