# OneBattery 1.2.2 (2026-10-30)

First released as **v1.1**; renumbered under Bradshaw Versioning on 2026-11-11.

## Real capacity measurement

Battery health now comes from measured charge, not the phone's own estimate.

- Counts the current flowing in during a charge and divides by how much the level rose, giving a real full-charge capacity.
- A background monitor with a small notification showing level, watts and temperature.
- The health card shows Measuring… with live progress until a charge of 15% or more completes.
- Charge cycles are hidden when the phone reports 0.

**Action required:** Allow notifications, and set OneBattery's battery usage to Unrestricted.

The source for this release was not kept, so this commit holds only these notes.
